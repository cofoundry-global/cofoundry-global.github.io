Why clients choose us

Traditional firms organize around departments. We organize around problems. As new information arrives, specialists rotate in precisely when their expertise creates the greatest value — patent attorneys beside computational chemists, manufacturing veterans reviewing software architectures.

On average, 9 of our 896 experts are active on your engagement at any moment. We consider this our most important number.
The model · Dynamic Expert Routing

The team is an output of the problem — recomputed continuously.

MECHANISM 01

Two Managing Partners, always

Every engagement is anchored by exactly two Managing Partners — never one, never three — attached from kickoff through implementation. One partner gives you an opinion. Two give you a decision.

MECHANISM 02

The Router

Our staffing platform scores your engagement's current state against the competence profile of all 896 experts, every week. When a dormant expert's marginal value crosses threshold, they are activated: briefed in 24 hours, contributing in 48. The Router proposes; your two partners confirm.

MECHANISM 03

Handback memos

Every deactivation produces a short written memo — what I found, what I changed, what I would watch. Memos accumulate, so a team whose membership changes weekly behaves like one that has worked together for years.

MECHANISM 04

Activation billing

You are billed only for activated expert-time plus a fixed partner retainer. Dormant experts cost nothing. Our incentive is sparsity: the best engagement is solved by the fewest, best-chosen people.

The roster

Exactly 896. Not one more, not one fewer.

The roster is closed and fixed in size. When an expert retires, exactly one is admitted — by unanimous consent of the Managing Partner pool, after a full-day adversarial review by nine serving experts chosen by the Router. There is no application process; the firm approaches you.

Why 896? The name does the arithmetic: seven cohorts of 128 experts each, one cohort convening every day of the week — the origin of our founding promise, seven days a week on Route 128. Large enough that no client problem falls outside the roster’s combined competence; small enough that every expert remains individually known to the partnership.
